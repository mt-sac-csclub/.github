# Community code of conduct

Mt. SAC Computer Science Club welcomes students of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Give credit, protect private information, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and disruptive behavior are not accepted in club spaces or repositories.

If you experience or witness a problem, contact **mtsac.csclub@gmail.com**. Club officers will review reports privately and take appropriate action.
